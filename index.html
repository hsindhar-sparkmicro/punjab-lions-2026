What to work on

  • 10 ducks in the DivX 30-over (bowled out for 75 and 76) and 10 ducks in the Premier T20.
  • Wides: 89 in 156.2 Premier T20 overs = 0.57 an over (about 10 a game). Other competitions 0.47–0.49.
  • T20 bowling depth: economy 6.74 (Premier T20) and 6.94 (DivX T20). After the top three, only 22 Premier T20 wickets from everyone else.
  • DivX 30-over run rate: economy 5.97 vs 4.80 in the Premier 40 → NRR −0.072.
  • Long-format results: both Premier 40 league losses came defending (222/7, 146/9); every DivX 30 chase that failed fell short.
  • Stretched squad: about 20 players across four teams; on 12 Sept both long-format sides played and both lost.

10For 2027

  1. Knockout batting plan

    Five down by halfway means consolidate. Darshpreet and Harjeet anchor the long format, not the T20 top order (SR under 90). In T20, lead with Abhinav, Harsimranjit and Pankaj (SR 145–170).

  2. Pick by format

    T20: Pankaj as finisher, Abhinav and Sujaan as strike bowlers. Long: Vahora and Humayun as main seamers (econ 5.06 and 5.15) plus Harsimranjit’s long spells. No T20 overs for bowlers going above 8.5.

  3. Cut the wides

    Under 6 a game in the Premier T20. Track Pankaj (20), Humayun (19), Pradeep and Jashan (11 each).

  4. Less reliance on the top two

    Develop a third batter averaging 25+ (Chiragdeep, Rowan and Lakhwinder showed signs in the DivX T20) and a fourth Premier T20 wicket-taker.

  5. Manage availability

    Lock the Premier core before September knockouts, avoid same-day double fixtures for key players, and plan around outside events (Sept 5–7, Oct 17–18).
